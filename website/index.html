THE ADVENTURE

Every path has
another layer.

Start with a shelter and a pickaxe. Follow branching caves into ruins, cross changing climates, meet neighbors, and bring home the resources to make your corner of the world your own.

Weather, day and night, water, wildlife and distinct regions make the journey feel different above ground and below it.

01 / EXPLORE

Go further

Descend through caves, discover landmarks and ruins, and travel from green forests to frost, desert and deeper regions.

02 / CREATE

Build a place to return to

Mine, smelt, craft, cook and furnish a shelter. Grow crops and help a settlement become a community.

03 / TOGETHER

Bring a friend

Play solo or in two-player co-op. Share storage and expeditions while keeping your own character, rewards and discoveries.
