THE GAME

One world.
Many ways to wander.

Follow branching caves into ruins, cross changing biomes, and bring what you find home. Mine and craft, build a shelter, fish and cook, meet neighbors, and prepare for encounters deeper underground.

Bramblevein is being built for solo play and two-player co-op. It is still in pre-1.0 development.

WHAT'S BEING WORKED ON

The world keeps growing.

The latest source work expands routes into Sky and Hell, adds more varied destinations and villages, and polishes the journal, combat feel and ultrawide presentation. These changes are in development; a public 1.0 release has not been announced.
